How AI Auto Video Cut Works

AI Auto Video Cut combines four AI steps in one pass: it selects the best moments from your clips and photos, applies a narration style or your own description, records a voice-over, and adds captions and music. Only the options you turn on run.

Create a Finished Video in 5 Steps

No timeline editor required, generate straight to a finished, downloadable video.

Step 1

Import Your Media

Add up to 60 videos or 300 photos, up to 30 minutes combined, with at least 30 seconds needed to generate. Tip: one photo counts as 5 seconds if you're short on length.

STEP2
Step 2

Describe Your Video, or Pick a Style

Write a line about what happened, or tap a style chip like Daily Vlog for an instant narration angle. Tip: specific details, names, places, give the AI more to work with.

STEP3
Step 3

Add a Voice-Over (Optional)

Turn Voice-Over on and pick a sample voice, or clone your own from a short recording. Tip: sample voices are free to preview, so try a few before generating.

STEP4
Step 4

Adjust Duration, Aspect Ratio, and Audio

Set duration up to 3 minutes, pick 9:16, 16:9, or 1:1, and toggle music, Smart Audio, web search, and photo motion. Tip: change aspect ratio early, it's only a recommendation from your first clip.

Step 5

Generate and Download

Generating runs in the background, so you can keep browsing while it renders; the finished video lands in your history. Tip: start a second project while the first one renders.
